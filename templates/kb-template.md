## 9. Escalation

Escalate when:

- The issue remains unresolved after the documented checks.
- Evidence indicates a problem outside the current support scope.
- A required change needs additional authorisation.
- Further troubleshooting could create unacceptable risk.

**Information to Include in an Escalation:**

- User impact and scope
- Checks already completed
- Relevant error messages and timestamps
- Confirmed findings and remaining hypotheses
- Changes made and verification results

Do not include passwords, tokens, or other secrets.
