## 3. Initial Assessment

**Clarifying Questions:**

- When did the issue start?
- What exact error message appeared?
- Has the task worked before?
- Are other users or devices affected?
- Were any relevant changes made recently?

**Initial Severity and Priority Rationale:**

Explain the impact and urgency. Do not assign priority based only on technical complexity.
